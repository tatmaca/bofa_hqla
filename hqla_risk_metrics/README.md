2025 University of Chicago MSFM Project Lab
Bank of America HQLA Risk Optimization

The HQLA Risk team is responsible for developing risk models
for key bank liquidity & profitability metrics and understanding
sensitivities of such metrics to different risk scenarios. Features
in this repo include:

- Attribution model linking various metrics to identified risk scenarios
- Underlying risk models to be fed into AI Monitoring Agent
- Portfolio allocation benchmarks to be fed into AI Monitoring Agent

This project is led by Amit Pandey and Adam Ashcraft at Bank of America
and overseen by Professor Amitabh Chaudhary at the University of Chicago.

The student collaborators on this project are Aryaa Gunavante, Charles
Benello, Josh Li, Togay Atmaca, and Xiangchen Liu.
