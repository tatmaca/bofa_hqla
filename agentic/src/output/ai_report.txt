**“HQLA Scenario Optimization & Rebalancing Analysis”**

1. **Executive Summary**
   - This exercise aims to optimize and rebalance the High-Quality Liquid Assets (HQLA) portfolio under four distinct scenarios.
   - The optimization method focuses on maximizing expected returns while adhering to liquidity coverage ratio (LCR) constraints.
   - The portfolio's LCR and allocation shifts vary across scenarios, reflecting the unique pressures each scenario presents.
   - The risk characterization of the portfolio is largely dependent on the scenario, with varying degrees of yield, liquidity stress, and curve shocks.

2. **Scenario Set Description**
   - **Lingering Inflation + Fed on Hold**
     - This scenario assumes a stall in Core PCE at 2.8% and a re-acceleration of services inflation, forcing the Fed to maintain rates at 5.50% into late 2025.
     - Probability: 0.32
     - Net Cash Outflow: $1,000,000,000
     - Unique pressures include higher yields and potential liquidity stress due to sustained inflation.
   - **Bull Steepener on Growth Scare**
     - This scenario anticipates a decline in payrolls and a collapse in small-business surveys, prompting an early sequence of Fed cuts.
     - Probability: 0.28
     - Net Cash Outflow: $1,000,000,000
     - Unique pressures include yield curve steepening and potential liquidity stress due to economic slowdown.
   - **Geopolitical Shock + Commodity Spike**
     - This scenario involves an energy supply disruption that lifts Brent above $110, rekindling breakevens and widening MBS basis.
     - Probability: 0.22
     - Net Cash Outflow: $1,000,000,000
     - Unique pressures include higher yields due to geopolitical risks and commodity price spikes.
   - **Reg Relief / Capital Recalibration**
     - This scenario assumes agencies finalize tweaks that soften G-SIB surcharge and Level 2 caps for HQLA.
     - Probability: 0.18
     - Net Cash Outflow: $1,000,000,000
     - Unique pressures include potential yield shifts due to regulatory changes.
